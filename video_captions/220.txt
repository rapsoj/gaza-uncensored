Young woman Raheel Mohammad Rasras
(32) died at Nasser Medical Complex in Khan Younis after battling severe malnutrition. Her weight dropped from 110 lbs (50 kg) to just 55 lbs (25 kg) due to hunger and the siege, until her frail body could no longer resist.
Raheel's death highlights the scale of the humanitarian catastrophe facing over two million people in Gaza.
